# Unfold

Folding your iPhone Duo locks the apps you choose. Unfolding unlocks them. Want in while it's folded? Open Unfold and flap the phone open and shut until it gives up.

## What it does

- **Fold to lock.** Picks apps with Screen Time and blocks them while the phone is closed.
- **Schedule.** Optionally only during certain hours and days. Outside them, folding does nothing.
- **Flap Unlock.** Close and reopen the phone a set number of times in Unfold to unlock for a while. The blocked-app screen links straight to it.
- **Shortcuts automations.** Display Closed → Lock Apps and Display Opened → Unlock Apps keep it working when Unfold isn't open. The app walks you through setting them up.

## Requirements

- iPhone Duo on iOS 27.1 or later
- Xcode 27.2
- An Apple developer team with the Family Controls capability. Change the signing team and bundle IDs to your own before building.
